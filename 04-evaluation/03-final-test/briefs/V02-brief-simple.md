# Problem Brief V02: Daecheong Reservoir

**Background.** The Daecheong multipurpose reservoir in the upper Geum River basin, central South Korea, serves flood control, water supply, in-stream flow and hydropower. Its flood-control storage is small: at the 200-year inflow it fills in about 6.5 hours. Every hour, operators issue a release schedule for the next several hours but carry out only its first step, and they rarely use optimisation tools. Rainfall forecasts cover only 6 hours, far shorter than many floods. Hourly records of storage, outflow and derived inflow are available for three long historical floods in 2016, 2018 and 2020, each lasting more than eight days with one to three peaks.

**Research question.** How can hourly release schedules be produced in real time that control these long floods and that operators would accept in practice? Design a study that answers this question.
