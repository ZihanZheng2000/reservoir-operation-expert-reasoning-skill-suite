# Problem Brief V05: Stânca–Costești Reservoir

**Background.** The Stânca–Costești reservoir on the Prut River, on the border between Romania and the Republic of Moldova, was built in 1973–1979 for flood control, hydropower, water supply, irrigation and fish farming. Its flood control volume is 665 mil. m³, but the 2008 flood brought about 1100 mil. m³ and a peak about 15% above the original design peak, and the reservoir rose above its flap gates. Releases are made through several bottom outlets and gated weir bays, each operated separately, and flood warnings give only about 3 days of lead time. Discharge records for 1978–2020 are available from an upstream gauge, whose high-flow rating curve is uncertain.

**Research question.** What design floods should the reservoir now be planned for, and how should each outlet be operated during a flood to keep the dam safe and limit flooding downstream? Design a study that answers these questions.
