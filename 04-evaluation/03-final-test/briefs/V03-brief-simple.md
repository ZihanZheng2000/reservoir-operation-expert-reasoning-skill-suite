# Problem Brief V03: Lishimen Reservoir

**Background.** Lishimen Reservoir on Shifeng Creek, in the Jiaojiang River basin of Zhejiang Province, East China, is operated mainly for flood control and irrigation, with water supply and hydropower as further uses. Typhoons bring intense rainfall to its small catchment, so floods rise and fall steeply and leave little warning time. Releases are set hour by hour, but reliable short-term inflow forecasts are not yet available, and any forecast will carry large errors. Hourly rainfall from six gauges for 1987-2020 and observed reservoir inflows for 22 flood events are available. The test case is the flood from Typhoon Rananim in August 2004.

**Research question.** How should hourly releases be set during a flood when the true inflow is not known in advance, and how much does forecast error affect flood safety upstream and downstream? Design a study that answers these questions and gives the agency one defensible operating plan.
