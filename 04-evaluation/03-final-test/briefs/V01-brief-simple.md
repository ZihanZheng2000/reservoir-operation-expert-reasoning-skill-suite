# Problem Brief V01: Three Gorges Reservoir

**Background.** The Three Gorges Reservoir on the Yangtze River, China, is operated mainly for flood control and hydropower. In the flood season its water level must normally stay at the design flood limit water level of 145 m. Current rules allow it to rise to 148 m when a 3-day inflow forecast supports this, but large volumes of water are still spilled each year. Daily inflow forecasts with lead times of 1 to 7 days, and the matching observed inflows, are available for the 2019 flood season.

**Research question.** How can these forecasts be used to hold the flood-season water level above 145 m in daily operation, and how much higher can it be held safely? Design a study that answers this question and shows whether the proposed operation is safe enough compared with current practice and how much it adds to hydropower.
